## Meeting Notes Summary: Discussion with Garmanee on Fintech in Myanmar

This document summarizes key insights and challenges discussed during a meeting with Garmanee regarding operating within the fintech sector in Myanmar.

### 1. Hurdles and Regulatory Requirements for Fintech Operations

Operating a fintech business in Myanmar presents several significant challenges:

*   **Licensing:** A Microfinance Business License (MFI) is required.
*   **Capital Requirement:** An upfront payment of 3 billion kyats is necessary.
*   **Banking Partnerships:** Essential to be partnered with a bank or have access to banking APIs.
*   **Monetization Strategy:** A clear plan for how the application will generate revenue is crucial.
*   **Central Bank Communication:** Communicating with the Central Bank of Myanmar (CBM) is often difficult and necessitates a local presence to manage interactions effectively.

### 2. Non-Bank Financial Institutions (NBFIs) and Deposit Holding

*   **Deposit Restrictions:** Non-bank financial institutions are legally prohibited from independently holding customer deposits.
*   **Sponsorship Requirement:** To operate, NBFIs must secure a partnership with a licensed financial sponsor (e.g., a bank).

### 3. Current Market Landscape: Payment Platforms and Bank Partnerships

The Myanmar digital payment market is characterized by strong partnerships between payment platforms and specific banks:

*   **Wave Money:** Digital payment service partnered with Yoma Bank.
*   **A+ Wallet:** Mobile banking and digital wallet service for A Bank.
*   **MoMoney:** Provides digital payments for Shwe Bank.

### 4. Dominance of KBZPay and its Monetization Strategy

KBZPay is the leading digital payment solution in Myanmar, with other banks struggling to compete effectively.

*   **Factors Contributing to Dominance:**
    *   **Merchant Discount Rate (MDR):** KBZPay does not charge an MDR percentage for smaller merchants, which significantly attracts and retains a large merchant base.
    *   **Monetization Strategy:** Their primary method of monetization involves:
        *   Utilizing their substantial holdings (billions of kyats) from millions of users.
        *   Deploying these funds for treasury investments.
        *   Engaging in commercial lending activities.

### 5. Strategic Positioning and Niche Opportunities

To succeed in the competitive Myanmar fintech market, a strategic approach is recommended:

*   **Niche Identification:** Focus on finding a specific market niche that is underserved.
*   **Target Smaller Banks:** Pitch solutions to smaller banks that currently lack their own robust payment platforms.
*   **Address Existing Issues:** Develop solutions that overcome common problems in current apps, such as:
    *   High friction in user experience.
    *   Security concerns.

### 6. Alternative Product Ideas

Beyond traditional fintech payment solutions, other product ideas were considered, particularly given Myanmar's sanction status:

*   **Office 365 Replacement:** Develop an alternative to Office 365, as Myanmar is sanctioned.
*   **Workflow/Project Management Tool:** Create a tool for workflow or project management.

### 7. General Recommendation

*   **Standardization:** Identify and implement standardized solutions from other countries into Myanmar, as there is a current lack of established standards in many areas.
